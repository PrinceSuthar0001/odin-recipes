kheer

Description

Kheer is a traditional South Asian rice pudding made by boiling milk, sugar, and rice (or broken wheat, tapioca, or vermicelli) until it reaches a thick, creamy consistency. It is a staple dessert in countries like India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh, celebrated for its rich flavor and cultural significance.

Ingredients

  • milk
  • grain rice
  • sugar

Steps

  1. Boil the Milk
  2. Cook the Rice
  3. Add Sugar and Flavor
  4. Add Nuts and Thicken
